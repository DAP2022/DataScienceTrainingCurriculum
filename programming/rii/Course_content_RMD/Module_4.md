# MODULE 4 — Hypothesis Testing: Binomial, Chi-Squared, t-Tests & Survival Analysis
### Question: How do we translate a biological question into a mathematically rigorous, code-based hypothesis test?
### Answer:  We establish a universal four-step framework for hypothesis testing and apply it to an escalating series of tests: from binomial proportions through goodness-of-fit, t-tests, and Kaplan-Meier survival curves

## Learning Objectives
•	Conduct one-sample, two-sample, and paired t-tests; identify the correct test for the experimental design.
•	Apply nonparametric alternatives (Wilcoxon test, Welch's approximate t-test) when normality assumptions are violated.
•	Construct and interpret Kaplan-Meier survival curves.

## In-Class Activities
•	Produce a Kaplan-Meier curve for the built-in leukemia dataset.
